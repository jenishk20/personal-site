I applied cold. In January a recruiter for the Production Engineering team wrote back to say she had reviewed my resume and thought I could be a good match. A month later I was out, rejected within an hour of the second screen round ending.

## What the role asks for

Production Engineers at Meta sit between software and systems engineering. The recruiter's note said the team wanted someone who knows Linux and Unix, enjoys debugging, and codes well. So I spent the weeks before revising operating systems, computer networks and databases in depth, not only LeetCode.

None of that was new to me. Those subjects are the core of GATE, India's graduate engineering entrance exam, which I sat three times, with a best rank of about 4,000.

## Within the hour

The rejection arrived within an hour of the second round. Fast enough that the decision was made from the scorecards almost as soon as they were in.

After that the recruiter stopped responding. That makes Meta the third recruiter on this site to go quiet, after <a href="/four-rounds-at-amazon-seattle-hq/">Amazon</a> and <a href="/two-rounds-at-bloomberg/">Bloomberg</a>.

## What I do not know

I cannot point to the moment it went wrong. I cannot recall a single point in either round where I went silent on a question. My own ratings put the systems round as the weaker of the two, and at the screen stage a Lean Hire may be all it takes. That is a guess.

That is what makes this the worst loop in my log, worse than the ones where I clearly failed. When I lose on a problem I could not solve, I know what to practise. Here I had prepared for exactly what was asked, and came away with no idea what to fix.
