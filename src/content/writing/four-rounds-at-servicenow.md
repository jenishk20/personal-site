## Three interviews in one afternoon

Rounds 2 to 4 all ran on Thursday 14 November: two back to back, a two hour gap, then the third. Thirty minutes each, and every one of them ended on my projects, so the same work got walked through three times for three different people.

Two things in it were unusual for an internship. The first interviewer opened on system design and the CAP theorem, which is a conversation most intern loops skip entirely. The last one followed a medium with questions on Docker and Kubernetes, which is to say on how software gets run rather than how it gets written.

## Why it ended there

Adobe's decision came first, and I accepted it in December. ServiceNow's results were slow to come back, and by the time they did I had already committed for the summer, so I stepped out of the process.

Three months later the same Adobe internship collided with another loop's timing, and at <a href="/four-rounds-at-dell/">Dell</a> it went the other way.
